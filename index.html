Family doctors urge tougher national conditions on tobacco smoking and vaping

The Malta College of Family Doctors and the Association of Private Family Doctors have urged the government to impose “tougher conditions” on smoking and vaping, noting Malta is the only EU member state holding out against the bloc’s Tobacco Taxation Directive. They said tobacco is behind a substantial share of cardiovascular, respiratory and cancer cases, which account for almost half of Malta’s annual mortality toll. Suggested measures include wider access to cessation medication and classes, more staff training in motivational interviewing, and more smoke-free areas.
